Class PCB assembly visualization, 2026-11-25.

Board geometry, copper and silkscreen: the instructor's KiCad project.
FireBeetle geometry: the supplied DFR0654.step, tessellated with FreeCAD 1.0.
The STEP X,Y,Z axes map to assembled Y,Z,X; the footprint location is
[38.862,45.974] mm relative to the PCB's bottom-left corner. Hole and pin
patterns match the footprint after that transform. Colors are illustrative.

Resistors, LEDs and pin headers: KiCad 7 standard 3D models placed using the
PCB footprint coordinates and rotations. U1 uses illustrative 1x18 and 1x14
vertical socket models; the module rests on their tops, 7.00024 mm above the
base PCB top. This fitted height must be checked against the lab hardware.

Library geometry: KiCad community, CC-BY-SA 4.0 with the electronic-design
exception described at https://www.kicad.org/libraries/license/ .
Model families: Resistor_THT/R_Axial_DIN0207_L6.3mm_D2.5mm_P10.16mm_Horizontal;
LED_THT/LED_D5.0mm; Connector_PinHeader_2.54mm/PinHeader_1x02 and 1x04;
Connector_PinSocket_2.54mm/PinSocket_1x14 and 1x18, all vertical 2.54 mm pitch.

The custom SW2 switch has no 3D model: its pads are visible, but its body is
not represented. OLED/SHT31 are shown as their board headers, not attached
sensor/display modules. Wires, fasteners and solder joints are omitted.
The assembled GLB is a teaching illustration, not a complete clearance check
or an object to print. Use the real board and parts to verify fit and values.

GLB dimensions are in metres, per glTF convention. The course viewer converts
them back to millimetres to align the separately loaded OpenSCAD stand.
