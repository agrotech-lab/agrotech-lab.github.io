Class PCB assembly visualization, 2026-11-25.

Board geometry, copper and silkscreen: the instructor's KiCad project.
FireBeetle geometry: the supplied DFR0654.step, tessellated with FreeCAD 1.0.
The STEP X,Y,Z axes map to assembled Y,Z,X; the footprint location is
[38.862,45.974] mm relative to the PCB's bottom-left corner. Hole and pin
patterns match the footprint after that transform. Colors are illustrative.

Resistors, LEDs and FireBeetle sockets: KiCad 7 standard 3D models placed using the
PCB footprint coordinates and rotations. U1 uses illustrative 1x18 and 1x14
vertical socket models; the module rests on their tops, 7.00024 mm above the
base PCB top. This fitted height must be checked against the lab hardware.

Library geometry: KiCad community, CC-BY-SA 4.0 with the electronic-design
exception described at https://www.kicad.org/libraries/license/ .
Model families: Resistor_THT/R_Axial_DIN0207_L6.3mm_D2.5mm_P10.16mm_Horizontal;
LED_THT/LED_D5.0mm;
Connector_PinSocket_2.54mm/PinSocket_1x14 and 1x18, all vertical 2.54 mm pitch.

OLED (J1), SHT31 (J2), thermistor (TH1), photoresistor (R2) and pushbutton (SW2):
original teaching geometry built by teaching-components.js. Their lead/pin
centres and net names come directly from the KiCad footprints, replacing the
placeholder header models. The source KiCad PCB and its footprints are unchanged.

The four-pin OLED follows the appearance of the lesson's archive/figures/OLED.png;
its 35.5 x 33 mm PCB is representative, not a measurement of the lab module.
The screen says AGROTECH / OLED as an illustrative display, not live readings.
The SHT31 uses a representative 13 x 14 mm four-pin breakout with a 2.5 x 2.5
x 0.9 mm sensor package, per Sensirion's specification:
https://sensirion.com/products/catalog/SHT31-DIS-B
The actual breakout board model has not been identified.

The black epoxy thermistor bead and light-facing photoresistor disc follow
archive/figures/thermistor.jpg and photoresistor.jpg. The thermistor type is
also illustrated in the course's thermistor-datasheet-NTCM-10K-B4150.pdf.
Bead/disc dimensions and formed lead heights are illustrative. The pushbutton
is a representative two-lead tactile body, located on SW2's two holes 5.08 mm
apart; this supersedes the generic switch symbol for the visualization only.

Module sizes, package shapes, colors, pin lengths and installed heights are
illustrative. Use the actual lab parts to check fit and connector pin order.
Wires, fasteners and solder joints are omitted.
The assembled GLB is a teaching illustration, not a complete clearance check
or an object to print. Use the real board and parts to verify fit and values.

GLB dimensions are in metres, per glTF convention. The course viewer converts
them back to millimetres to align the separately loaded OpenSCAD stand.
